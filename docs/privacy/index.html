1. Who we are and what this policy covers

DROPLINE is a portrait mobile pipe-connection puzzle game published by Tonaroz Team. The game includes a progression of puzzle levels and customization features. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game and related services. Actual practices depend on the features and third-party software included in the version you install.

Developer: Tonaroz Team
Privacy and support contact: Tunarozteam@gmail.com

2. Game information and device data

The documented game design includes levels, daily puzzles, player progress, coins, hints, settings and cosmetic selections. Gameplay values and preferences may be stored locally on your device so the game can operate and remember your progress.

The data collected by a particular release may also depend on analytics, crash-reporting, cloud-save, account, purchase or other software components enabled in that release. Tonaroz Team must identify the actual components and update this notice if they collect additional information. Do not assume that every version has the same data practices.

3. Advertising and third-party providers

Tonaroz Team's project documentation identifies Google AdMob as the intended advertising service. Whether AdMob or mediation partners are active depends on the released build and its configuration. If Google Mobile Ads SDK is included, Google may collect and process information as described in its SDK disclosures. Depending on the SDK version, features and configuration, this may include IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, device or advertising identifiers, and information used for advertising, analytics, security or fraud prevention.

Google resources: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Unity · Google Privacy Policy.

If mediation or other third-party SDKs are enabled, those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. The developer should list the providers actually included in the release and review their data disclosures.

4. Consent and privacy controls

The project documentation identifies Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) as the intended consent-management solution. The exact implementation and available controls depend on the production build. Where UMP is used, it should be configured to refresh consent information, display messages required for the user's region, and determine whether an ad request is permitted.

Where consent is required by applicable law, it must be obtained before the relevant processing or ad request. Your choices should be respected. A privacy-options entry point should be provided in the app when required by the SDK, platform policy or applicable law. Actual consent flows, regions and advertising choices must match the production configuration.

Learn more: UMP for Unity — Google developer documentation.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Gameplay progress and settings stored only on your device generally remain there until you clear the app's data, reset the game or uninstall it, subject to the operating system and the app's implementation. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete information already processed by an advertising or other third-party provider.

Retention of information handled by Google or other providers is governed by their policies and applicable service settings. If the game uses accounts, cloud saves or server-side records, additional retention and deletion arrangements may apply and should be explained in an updated policy.

For a privacy request to Tonaroz Team, email Tunarozteam@gmail.com. Include “DROPLINE Privacy Request” in the subject and describe your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

6. Data security

Tonaroz Team aims to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services actually operated. Information transmitted over networks should be protected using appropriate transport security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should keep their devices and operating systems up to date.

7. Children's privacy and audience settings

The target age groups for DROPLINE must be set accurately in Google Play Console. This policy does not by itself designate the game as child-directed or as suitable for every age group.

If children or users of unknown age may receive ads, the app and its advertising setup must follow applicable Google Play Families requirements. These can include using eligible self-certified ad SDK versions, age-appropriate ad content and formats, and restrictions on personalized advertising and identifiers. Mixed-audience apps may require a neutral age screen. The actual target audience and ad configuration must be reviewed before release.

References: Google Play Families Policy · Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program.

Requests concerning information held by Google or another provider may also need to be submitted through that provider's privacy tools.
