1. Who we are and what this policy covers

DROPLINE is a mobile pipe-connection puzzle game published by Tonaroz Team. This policy describes how information may be handled when you use the game and related services. The actual practices depend on the features and third-party SDKs included in the version you install.

Developer: Tonaroz Team
Privacy and support contact: Tunarozteam@gmail.com

2. Game information and device data

The documented game design includes levels, daily puzzles, progress, coins, hints, settings and cosmetic selections. These gameplay values and preferences may be stored locally on your device so the game can operate and remember your progress.

This policy does not claim that the released app collects only local data or that it collects no personal data. If analytics, crash reporting, cloud saves, accounts, purchases, or other SDKs are included in a release, their data types, purposes, recipients and retention periods must be added here before publication.

3. Advertising and third-party providers

The project documentation describes Google AdMob as an intended advertising service. AdMob integration and any mediation partners must be confirmed in the actual release build. If Google Mobile Ads SDK is included, Google may collect and share information described in its SDK disclosures. Depending on SDK version, features and configuration, this can include IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, device or advertising identifiers, and information used for advertising, analytics, security or fraud prevention. The precise data categories must be checked against the version shipped.

Google's information: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Unity · Google Privacy Policy.

If mediation or other third-party SDKs are enabled, the developer must list each provider, link its privacy policy, and disclose the data it receives. Third-party providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

4. Consent and privacy controls

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is documented as an intended consent-management option, but its installation and correct operation have not yet been verified. If UMP is used, the app should refresh consent information at launch, show required messages, check whether ads may be requested, and provide an in-app privacy-options entry point when required by the SDK or applicable rules.

Where consent is legally required, the app must obtain it before the relevant processing or ad request. Your choices should be respected and a required privacy choice should remain accessible from the app's settings. The actual consent flow, regions, ad personalization settings and available controls must match the production configuration.

Learn more: UMP for Unity — Google developer documentation.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Gameplay progress and settings stored only on your device generally remain there until you clear the app's data, reset the game, or uninstall it, subject to the operating system and the app's implementation. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete information already processed by an advertising or other third-party provider.

Retention of information handled by Google or other providers is governed by their policies and the settings applicable to the service. If the game adds accounts, cloud saves or server-side records, Tonaroz Team must publish a clear deletion method and applicable retention periods before enabling those features.

For a privacy request to Tonaroz Team, email Tunarozteam@gmail.com. Include “DROPLINE Privacy Request” in the subject and describe your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

6. Data security

Tonaroz Team aims to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services actually operated. Data sent over networks should be protected using appropriate transport security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. The developer must also protect credentials and signing keys and must not publish secrets in source repositories.

7. Children's privacy and audience settings

The game's target age groups have not been confirmed in the project information reviewed for this page. This policy therefore does not state that DROPLINE is child-directed or that it is suitable for every age. The developer must set the target audience accurately in Google Play Console before release.

If children or users of unknown age may receive ads, the developer must apply the relevant Google Play Families requirements, including use of eligible self-certified ad SDK versions, age-appropriate ad content and formats, and restrictions on personalized advertising and identifiers. Mixed audiences may require neutral age screening. Do not publish until the target audience and ad configuration have been reviewed.

References: Google Play Families Policy · Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program.

8. Your privacy rights and requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection regarding personal information. Contact Tonaroz Team using the address below. We may need reasonable information to identify the relevant request and will respond as required by applicable law. Requests concerning data held by Google or another provider may also need to be submitted through that provider's privacy tools.

Google privacy controls: Google My Ad Center · Google Account data and privacy.

This policy may change when the game, SDKs, advertising configuration or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this address with a revised update date.

عندما تكون الموافقة مطلوبة قانونياً، يجب الحصول عليها قبل المعالجة المعنية أو طلب الإعلان. وينبغي احترام اختياراتك وإتاحة الوصول مجدداً إلى خيارات الخصوصية المطلوبة من إعدادات اللعبة. ويجب أن تتطابق آلية الموافقة والمناطق وإعدادات تخصيص الإعلانات والخيارات المتاحة مع إعدادات نسخة الإنتاج الفعلية.
