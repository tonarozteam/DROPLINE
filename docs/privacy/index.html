Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

DROPLINE is a portrait mobile pipe-connection puzzle game published by Tonaroz Team. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game and related services. Actual practices depend on the features and third-party software included in the version you install.

Developer: Tonaroz Team
Privacy and support: Tunarozteam@gmail.com

2. Game Information and Device Data

The game includes levels, daily puzzles, player progress, coins, hints, settings and cosmetic selections. Gameplay values and preferences may be stored locally on your device so the game can operate and remember your progress.

Data processed by a particular release may also depend on analytics, crash-reporting, cloud-save, account, purchase or other software components enabled in that release. The data practices may therefore differ between versions.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Providers

Tonaroz Team's project documentation identifies Google AdMob as the intended advertising service. Whether AdMob or mediation partners are active depends on the released build and its configuration. If the Google Mobile Ads SDK is included, Google may collect and process information as described in its SDK disclosures. Depending on the SDK version, features and configuration, this may include IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, device or advertising identifiers, and information used for advertising, analytics, security or fraud prevention.

Google resources: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Unity · Google Privacy Policy.

If mediation or other third-party SDKs are enabled, those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

4. Consent and Privacy Controls

The project documentation identifies Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) as the intended consent-management solution. The exact implementation and available controls depend on the production build. Where UMP is used, it should be configured to display messages required for the user's region and determine whether an ad request is permitted.

Where consent is required by applicable law, it must be obtained before the relevant processing or ad request. Your choices should be respected. A privacy-options entry point should be provided in the app when required by the SDK, platform policy or applicable law.

Learn more: UMP for Unity — Google developer documentation.

5. Storage, Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and settings stored only on your device generally remain there until you clear the app's data, reset the game or uninstall it, subject to the operating system and the app's implementation. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete information already processed by an advertising or other third-party provider.

Retention of information handled by Google or other providers is governed by their policies and applicable service settings. If the game uses accounts, cloud saves or server-side records, additional retention and deletion arrangements may apply.

For a privacy request to Tonaroz Team, email Tunarozteam@gmail.com with “DROPLINE Privacy Request” in the subject. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

6. Data Security

Tonaroz Team aims to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services actually operated. Information transmitted over networks should be protected using appropriate transport security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The target age groups for DROPLINE must be set accurately in Google Play Console. This policy does not by itself designate the game as child-directed or suitable for every age group.

If children or users of unknown age may receive ads, the app and its advertising setup must follow applicable Google Play Families requirements. These can include using eligible ad SDK versions, age-appropriate ad content and formats, and restrictions on personalized advertising and identifiers. The actual target audience and ad configuration must be reviewed before release.

References: Google Play Families Policy · Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program.
