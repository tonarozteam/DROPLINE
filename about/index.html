The game

DROPLINE challenges players to rotate and connect pipe segments across 2,000 levels in five worlds.

The game is designed for portrait Android devices and includes pipe skins, board backgrounds, a cosmetics shop, hints and player progression.

Worlds

  • Beginner: Levels 1–400
  • Explorer: Levels 401–800
  • Challenger: Levels 801–1,200
  • Expert: Levels 1,201–1,600
  • Master: Levels 1,601–2,000
