1. Who We Are

DROPLINE is a portrait mobile pipe-connection puzzle game published by Tonaroz Team. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game and related services.

Developer: Tonaroz Team
Privacy and support: Tunarozteam@gmail.com

2. Game Information and Device Data

The game includes levels, daily puzzles, player progress, coins, hints, settings and cosmetic selections. Gameplay values and preferences may be stored locally on your device so the game can operate and remember your progress.

When the game connects to third-party services, those services may receive technical information needed to provide, secure and improve their services. The exact information depends on the service, SDK version, device settings and configuration.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The DROPLINE Android application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The current game implementation includes optional rewarded ads, which offer in-game coins or hints after the required ad-completion event, and interstitial ads at selected level transitions. The current implementation does not include banner or app-open ads.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers (where available and permitted), app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostics, and information used for security, fraud prevention, measurement and advertising. The data collected in a particular session depends on Google's SDK behavior, your device settings, consent status, regional requirements and the configuration of the released app.

Google resources: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Unity · Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and Privacy Controls

DROPLINE integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to support privacy and consent messages. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, UMP may display a consent message before ad requests are allowed. The game checks the consent state before requesting ads.

You can open Privacy Choices in the game's Settings to review or change the choices made available for your region. Some regions or consent states may not require a form to be shown. Changing a choice may affect whether ads can be requested.

Learn more: UMP for Unity — Google developer documentation.

5. Storage, Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and settings stored locally generally remain on your device until you clear the app's data, reset the game or uninstall it, subject to Android and the app's implementation. Uninstalling the game does not necessarily delete information already processed by Google or another third-party provider.

Google and other providers determine retention for information they process under their own policies and service settings. For a privacy request to Tonaroz Team, email Tunarozteam@gmail.com with “DROPLINE Privacy Request” in the subject. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

6. Data Security

Tonaroz Team aims to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services operated. Information transmitted over networks should be protected using appropriate transport security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy and Age Groups

The target age groups for DROPLINE are determined by the audience and content declarations published for the app in Google Play. This policy does not independently assign an age rating or state that the game is directed to children.

If the app is made available to children or users of unknown age, the game and its advertising configuration must comply with applicable laws and Google Play Families requirements before ads are served to those users. These requirements may include restrictions on identifiers, personalized advertising, ad content and eligible SDKs.

References: Google Play Families Policy · Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection regarding personal information.
