Current app availability

The current version of DROPLINE may not include an active advertising network or an in-app privacy choices control. If the app displays a message that privacy choices are not available, it means there are no advertising consent choices to manage in that version.

When advertising is enabled

If advertising is introduced in a future version, DROPLINE may use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), depending on the release configuration and regional requirements. Where applicable, a consent message will explain the relevant choices before advertising-related processing takes place.

Changing your choices

If a privacy options entry is available in the game settings, use it to review or change the choices offered for your region. The available options depend on the applicable requirements and the consent tools enabled in that version of the app.

For questions about privacy choices in DROPLINE, contact Tunarozteam@gmail.com. For more information about data handling, read the Privacy Policy.
